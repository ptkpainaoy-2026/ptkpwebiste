About PTKP Aina oy

PTKP Aina oy is a Finnish limited liability company based in Vantaa, offering a diverse range of services to support residential, commercial and operational needs.

Our activities include construction, carpentry, plumbing, surface finishing, electrical installation and maintenance, and cleaning services.

Our Main Services

We provide a wide range of services to meet your needs.

Construction & Renovation

Plumbing repairs and installations for homes and business premises.

Painting & Surface Finishing

Electrical Installation & Maintenance
